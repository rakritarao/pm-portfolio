about me

Where product meets technology

Hi, I'm Rakrita. I'm a Computer Science and Business Management student who enjoys the part of building where user needs, technical constraints, and business decisions all have to meet.

My technical background helps me understand what goes into building a product, but I'm especially interested in the questions around it: Who are we building for? What is actually frustrating them? Which problem is worth solving first? And how do we know the solution made things better?

That mindset has shaped the way I approach everything from testing a two-sided food rescue platform to translating AI findings into business recommendations and supporting real users at Stony Brook.
